## Key question 1. Which drivers are documented by the actors' own words or by financial/polling data, and which are only inferred by critics?

### Takeaway
Four drivers are documented in the actors' own words or in hard data: (i) electoral message-testing and ad buying (American Principles Project memos and on-record remarks; AdImpact counts), (ii) an explicit movement framing strategy (Rufo's published speech; Project 2025 text; leaked 2019–20 advocates' emails), (iii) favourable polling on parental notification and on minors' medical transition, and (iv) since January 2025, federal executive orders and enforcement announcements. A genuine factual grievance (real nondisclosure policies, several record-backed concealment or continuation-over-objection cases, the Cass Review, European restrictions, a first detransitioner verdict) is also documented. By contrast, claims that the messaging is driven by donor control, by fundraising revenue, by platform payouts, or by foreign amplification rest on watchdog assertion or inference: no document found shows a donor directing the message, organization revenue cannot be attributed to this topic, no account-level earnings were found, and no influence-operation report was found that concerns gender-in-schools specifically.

### Inferences
- (e) The best-documented driver is electoral: one organization (APP) has said on the record, repeatedly and over several years, that it tested messages to find one candidates would use, measured vote shifts, and distributed the results to campaigns. - (e) The leaked 2019–20 emails document coordination on medical-ban legislation and deliberate word choice. They do not document a plan to claim that schools medicate children; critics who cite them for that proposition go beyond the text as reported.
- (e) Polling gives politicians a real incentive on two narrow propositions (parents should be told; minors' medical transition should be restricted) and no measured incentive on the strong claim, because no poll found tests it.
- (e) "Fundraising drives the messaging" is plausible but undocumented: revenue grew at several organizations on both sides, but none reports revenue by issue and no fundraising email built on the school-medication claim was retrieved.
- (e) "Platform monetization drives the messaging" is plausible in general (an impressions-based payout existed on X from 2023 to 2026) but undocumented for any account that posts this content.

## Key question 2. How large is the measurable money (ads, organizational budgets, fee awards), by year?

## Key question 3. What is the relationship between the well-supported claim (some schools did not tell parents about social transition) and the unsupported one (schools arrange medication or surgery) in strategy documents and polling — do strategists distinguish them?

### Takeaway
The legal organizations and the enforcement documents keep the claims apart: ADF, the Education Department's letters and every federal investigation described in the sources allege social transition, records and non-notification only, and one executive order even defines "social transition" to exclude "chemical or surgical mutilation." The two claims are fused mainly in political communication: the same executive order's purpose clause, Project 2025's sentence on "social or medical gender transition without parental consent," campaign ads, and President Trump's rally statements, for which his campaign and White House twice offered notification policies and social-transition cases as the supporting evidence. Polls measure support for notification and for restricting minors' medical care separately; no poll was found that measures belief in the school-medication claim.

### Cited Findings
- **(a) Fused in one sentence, then separated by definition — EO 14190 (2025-01-29):** the purpose section says "steering students toward surgical and chemical mutilation without parental consent or involvement… may contravene Federal laws that protect parental rights," while the definitions say "'Social transition' does not include chemical or surgical mutilation," and the operative funding provision targets "the social transition of a minor student, including through school staff or teachers or through deliberately concealing the minor's social transition from the minor's parents." — [EO 14190, Federal Register via govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-02-03/html/2025-02232.htm)
- **(a) Fused — Project 2025:** "schools could be required to assist a child with a social or medical gender transition without parental consent." — [Mandate for Leadership, p. 343](https://static.heritage.org/project2025/2025_MandateForLeadership_FULL.pdf)
- **(a) Kept apart — ADF:** Waggoner's 2025 piece alleges secret *social* transition only and mentions surgery only to rebut critics; it bridges the two by arguing social transition "is a form of medical treatment." — [ADF](https://adflegal.org/article/kristen-waggoner-secret-social-transitions-are-all-too-real-and-theyre-hurting-kids/). The Kettle Moraine court adopted that bridge, holding the district's practice "without parental consent violates parents' constitutional right to determine the appropriate medical and healthcare for their children"; the First Circuit rejected the characterization in *Foote* (catalog SOC-US-003). — [Decision and Order](https://assets.adflegal.org/archive/adfm/TF-Decision.pdf)
- **(a) Kept apart — Education Department:** Secretary McMahon's 2025-03-28 letter conceded "FERPA does not provide an affirmative obligation for school officials to inform parents about any information." — [Education Week, 2025-03-28](https://www.edweek.org/policy-politics/mcmahon-says-schools-with-gender-plans-could-be-violating-federal-privacy-law/2025/03). Education Week's September 2026 survey of the investigations "does not describe medical treatment in any of the cases." — [Education Week, 2026-09-17](https://www.edweek.org/policy-politics/trump-admin-turns-to-student-privacy-laws-in-gender-identity-fights-with-schools/2026/09)
- **(a)/(c) Fused — presidential statements:** 2024-08-30, Moms for Liberty event: "your kid goes to school and comes home a few days later with an operation. The school decides what's going to happen with your child." — [PolitiFact](https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2024/sep/05/donald-trump/trumps-pants-on-fire-claim-that-schools-are-perfor). 2026-10-16, Dalton, Georgia: "we have five states where your child goes off to school as Tim and comes back as Tammy, three or four days later… They don't even need approval." — [CNN via KEYT](https://keyt.com/politics/cnn-us-politics/2026/10/20/fact-check-trump-brings-back-his-2024-lie-that-schools-are-secretly-sending-kids-to-hospitals-for-gender-surgery/)
- **(c) The evidentiary substitution:** in 2024 the campaign cited notification policies; in 2026 the White House sent social-transition cases; in both instances fact-checkers found no case of school-arranged surgery. — [PolitiFact](https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2024/sep/05/donald-trump/trumps-pants-on-fire-claim-that-schools-are-perfor); [CNN via KEYT](https://keyt.com/politics/cnn-us-politics/2026/10/20/fact-check-trump-brings-back-his-2024-lie-that-schools-are-secretly-sending-kids-to-hospitals-for-gender-surgery/)
- **(a) APP moves from a notification ruling to a medical demand:** responding to *Mirabelli* (a nondisclosure case), Schilling said "Parents need lawmakers to step up and pass nationwide bans on the poison of child sex changes." — [Center Square via WJLA](https://wjla.com/news/nation-world/parents-rights-advocates-hail-scotus-ruling-against-secret-gender-transitions-california-law-governor-gavin-newsom-schools-students-children-teenagers)
- **(a) What strategists say they tested:** APP's 2019 polling compared bathrooms with sports (sports won); its 2026 memo reports tests of sports and spaces, tax-funded "sex changes," minors' transitions, and a violence message. School secrecy is not listed among tested messages in either. — [repost of NYT article](https://thenewstalkers.com/community/discussion/61143/how-a-campaign-against-transgender-rights-mobilized-conservatives); [APP memo](https://americanprinciplesproject.s3.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2026-APP-Candidate-Memo.pdf)
- **(c) What the ads say:** AP's description of 2026 ads: minors' "sex-reassignment surgery," a detransitioner testimonial, sports, and an ad accusing a candidate of "backing child abuse charges against parents who do not support transitioning their children"; PolitiFact rated a Senate Leadership Fund ad false. Schools are not mentioned. — [AP via News4Jax](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2026/10/28/republicans-emphasize-transgender-ads-putting-cultural-issues-over-voters-economic-anxiety/). The 2024 Trump ads concerned taxpayer-funded transitions for prisoners and detainees. — [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-anti-trans-ads-spending)
- **(b) Polling keeps them apart:** notification items (AP-NORC about 40% approve with a neutral option; HarrisX 80–94% by party; Rasmussen California 69%) and medical items (Pew 56% favor a ban on care for minors; HarrisX 40–75% "never permitted" by party) are asked separately. Only HarrisX offered "without parental consent" as an option for minors' treatment: 16% of Democrats chose it. — [Yahoo/The Hill](https://www.yahoo.com/news/us-adults-approve-more-trump-024217114.html); [Deseret](https://www.deseret.com/politics/2024/10/24/parental-rights-trump-harris-transgender-education-pronouns); [Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/02/26/americans-have-grown-more-supportive-of-restrictions-for-trans-people-in-recent-years/); [Rasmussen](https://www.rasmussenreports.com/public_content/politics/partner_surveys/california_voters_support_parental_rights_by_overwhelming_margins)
- **(a) A documented bridge used by advocates:** the Cass Review's finding that early social transition is associated with proceeding to a medical pathway, and Waggoner's claim that "desistance rates collapse in the face of social transition" (no study named), are the stated basis for treating school social transition as the first step to medication. — [ADF](https://adflegal.org/article/kristen-waggoner-secret-social-transitions-are-all-too-real-and-theyre-hurting-kids/)
- **(b) What the record shows about that bridge:** in the catalog's cases any medication was prescribed by clinicians under parental consent, a minor-consent statute or a court order, and a state expert testified "in order to get to gender clinic, you have to be out to at least one caregiver." — [*Mirabelli* MSJ order, p. 25](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/63d954d4e4ad424df7819d46/6949e11ea6bae817c8eaf637_Dkt%20%20307_MSJ%20Order.pdf)

### Inferences
- (e) The distinction tracks audience and accountability: documents that must survive a court or an administrative record (complaints, briefs, findings letters) allege only what the files show; rally speech and 30-second ads do not carry that constraint.
- (e) The strong claim may persist because the weak claim is true often enough to be cited when challenged; the two documented substitutions (2024 and 2026) are consistent with that, but no strategist has said so.
- (e) Strategists' own tests suggest the persuasive weight sits with sports and minors' medical care, not with school secrecy; school secrecy functions more as the litigation and governing agenda than as the tested ad message. This is an inference from what the APP documents list, not a statement by APP.

### Gaps
- No poll was found that asks whether respondents believe schools perform or arrange surgery or hormones; one search returned none and the follow-up search was blocked by the cap.
- No internal campaign document was found discussing whether to make or avoid the surgery-at-school claim.
- Advertising content data (share of trans-themed ads that mention schools or parents) were not found.

## Key question 4. What changed after January 2025, when the messaging gained federal executive backing?

### Takeaway
From January 2025 the claims acquired official text, enforcement machinery and a federal megaphone: three executive orders, a March 2025 FERPA letter, a growing list of investigations (at least 11 by March 2025; new batches through November 2026), a FERPA finding against California with a funding threat near $5 billion, Justice Department suits, an FBI tip request and an HHS evidence review. The strongest official language ("steering students toward surgical and chemical mutilation without parental consent," districts "secretly 'transitioning' students") goes further than what the findings establish: the described allegations concern names, pronouns, support plans and records; outside experts say the California finding identified no specific denial of records; and no federal document alleges school-arranged medication.

### Cited Findings
- **(a) EO 14168 (signed 2025-01-20):** defines "gender ideology" as replacing "the biological category of sex with an ever-shifting concept of self-assessed gender identity" and orders agencies to "end the Federal funding of gender ideology." — [Federal Register via govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-01-30/html/2025-02090.htm)
- **(a) EO 14187 (2025-01-28):** "medical professionals are maiming and sterilizing a growing number of impressionable children"; "Countless children soon regret that they have been mutilated"; defines "child" as under 19; directs HHS to publish an evidence review within 90 days and agencies to rescind reliance on WPATH. It does not mention schools. — [Federal Register via govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-02-03/html/2025-02194.htm)
- **(a) EO 14190 (2025-01-29):** "parents have witnessed schools indoctrinate their children in radical, anti-American ideologies while deliberately blocking parental oversight"; "young men and women are made to question whether they were born in the wrong body and whether to view their parents and their reality as enemies"; directs an "Ending Indoctrination Strategy" and tells the Attorney General to coordinate with state and local prosecutors to "file appropriate actions against K-12 teachers and school officials who violate the law by: (i) sexually exploiting minors; (ii) unlawfully practicing medicine by offering diagnoses and treatment without the requisite license; or (iii) otherwise unlawfully facilitating the social transition of a minor student." — [Federal Register via govinfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2025-02-03/html/2025-02232.htm)
