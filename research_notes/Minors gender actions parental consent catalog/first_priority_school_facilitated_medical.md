# First-priority question: has any U.S. school or school employee arranged or materially facilitated gender-related medication or surgery for a minor without parental knowledge/consent and without a prior court order?

Status checked through 2026-11-18 (source dates given per item). Scope: nationwide, extra attention to California. "Four elements" used below: (1) a school/school employee/SBHC/district contractor arranged or materially facilitated; (2) gender-related MEDICATION (blockers, cross-sex hormones) or SURGERY that was actually prescribed/dispensed/administered/performed; (3) the relevant parent/guardian was unaware or had not consented; (4) no prior court order (or agency custody authority) authorized the treatment. Chest binders are recorded as a separate "device" category.

Evidence labels used: **established by records** / **corroborated account** / **sworn but contested allegation** / **reported allegation** / **unresolved**.

Access limits to note: these pages returned 403/451/empty and could NOT be read directly — U.S. Dept. of Education press releases (ed.gov), LGBTQ Nation, Daily Signal SBHC "investigation" (2024-03-08), mass.gov DCF gender-affirming medication consent policy PDF, CNN's own site (read via syndication instead), Catholic Free Press, CourtListener opinion pages, justia regulations. Juvenile/dependency court files (Yaeli; Kutzko) are confidential/sealed and were not available.

---

## Key Question 1: Does any case satisfy all four elements? If not, which case comes closest and exactly which element(s) fail or are unproven?

### Takeaway
No case located satisfies all four elements: in every candidate traced to records, either no medication/surgery occurred (most cases are social transition, counseling, information/brochures, or a chest binder), or the treatment was authorized by a parent, by a juvenile court/child-welfare custody process, or occurred after age 18. The closest are (a) *Lavigne* (Maine) — a school social worker allegedly handed a 13-year-old two chest binders without telling parents, but that is a device, the allegation was never adjudicated, and the case ended with cert denied 2026-03-30; (b) Yaeli Galdamez/Abigail Martinez (Arcadia, CA) — testosterone as a minor with alleged school-psychologist advocacy, but on the mother's own account a judge authorized the hormones while the teen was a DCFS dependent; and (c) Kutzko (Massachusetts, 2025-26) — school counseling plus a mandated report preceded DCF custody, DCF reportedly sought testosterone, but a juvenile court blocked it and no administration is established. This is a description of what the searches found, not proof that no qualifying case exists.

#### B. Candidate cases, element by element

**B1. *Lavigne v. Great Salt Bay Community School Board* (Damariscotta/Newcastle, Maine) — DEVICE (chest binder), not medication/surgery**
- *What the school employee allegedly did:* Per the First Circuit's recitation of the complaint, in December 2022 the mother found a chest binder in her 13-year-old's room; the child said school social worker Samuel Roy "provided the chest binder and instructed A.B. on how to use it," gave a second binder the same day, and said "he was not going to tell A.B.'[s] parents … and A.B. need not do so either." School staff also used a different name/pronouns. — First Circuit opinion (Montecalvo, J., filed 2025-07-28), reproduced at App. 3a–5a of the [cert petition, No. 25-759](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-759/390158/20251222174317126_Cert%20Petition.pdf)
- *Label:* **reported allegation** (unsworn complaint allegations, accepted as true only for motion-to-dismiss purposes; the school's opposition notes the "not going to tell" statement is pleaded on "information and belief"; no fact-finding ever occurred). — [Brief in Opposition, 2026-02-23, pp. 3–4](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-759/397047/20260223142651745_25-759BriefInOpposition.pdf)
- *Treatment:* none. No medication or surgery alleged. Device category only.
- *Parental involvement:* **parents not notified** (as alleged). Mother held ordinary parental authority; no court order or agency custody involved.
- *School response:* Superintendent allegedly said "no policy had been violated"; the Board's 2023-01-14 statement cited Maine minors' right to access mental-health services without parental consent (22 MRSA §1502) and confidential counseling with school-based providers (20-A MRSA §4008), and said it was not aware of any violation of policy or law. — [Brief in Opposition, pp. 4–6](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-759/397047/20260223142651745_25-759BriefInOpposition.pdf)
- *Rulings:* D. Me. (Levy, J.) dismissed claims vs. individuals, then the remaining Board claim on 2024-05-03 — [Maine Public, 2024-05-06](https://www.mainepublic.org/education-news/2024-05-06/a-judge-has-dismissed-a-parents-lawsuit-against-a-school-over-her-childs-gender-transition). First Circuit affirmed 2025-07-28 (146 F.4th 115) on the ground that the complaint did not plausibly allege a Board policy/custom of withholding (municipal liability), noting the guidelines "do not explicitly address 'the giving of chest binders or any other devices to students'" — it did not hold that the alleged conduct was constitutional. — [cert petition appendix, App. 19a–20a](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-759/390158/20251222174317126_Cert%20Petition.pdf); [Bangor Daily News, 2025-07-29](https://www.bangordailynews.com/2025/07/29/midcoast/midcoast-police-courts/appeals-court-rules-against-maine-mother-lawsuit-school-concealed-child-gender-transition)
- *Current status (checked 2026-11-18):* cert petition filed 2025-12-22; response requested 2026-01-22; **Petition DENIED 2026-03-30**. Final; no judgment on the facts, no settlement. — [Supreme Court docket No. 25-759](https://www.supremecourt.gov/docket/docketfiles/html/public/25-759.html)
- *Elements:* (1) school employee conduct — alleged, direct; (2) medication/surgery — **fails** (device); (3) parent unaware — alleged; (4) no court order — satisfied.

**B2. Yaeli Galdamez / Abigail Martinez (Arcadia Unified, Los Angeles County, CA) — hormones as a minor, but court/agency-authorized on the mother's own account**
- *Source of the account:* almost entirely the mother (interviews with Daily Mail/Daily Wire/Epoch Times/Heritage; amicus briefs filed for her by First Liberty and ADF). Repeated coverage traces back to her; it is not independent corroboration. Juvenile dependency records are confidential and were not found.
- *What school staff allegedly did:* "School staff told Yaeli to clandestinely join the LGBTQ club"; "The school psychologist pushing Yaeli's gender transition told her to accuse her mother of abuse at the police station, which would allow the state to pay for Yaeli's gender transition without parental consent." — [Brief of Abigail Martinez as amicus, No. 25-840, filed 2026-02-17, pp. 2–3](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-840/396397/20260217122626997_25-840%20Brief.pdf). No source alleges that school staff scheduled, prescribed, dispensed or transported for hormones.
- *Custody:* DCFS placed her in a group home at 16 and placed the mother on a child-abuse registry; the mother says she was later "absolved of all claims of abuse and removed from the child abuse registry." — [same brief, pp. 2–4](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-840/396397/20260217122626997_25-840%20Brief.pdf). The removal is dated April 2016 in a federal-court order's description of the complaint (search-result summary; page itself unreadable). — [CourtListener listing](https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/9512300/the-estate-of-yaeli-mozzelle-galdamez-v-county-of-los-angeles/)
- *Treatment and authorization:* "Siding with the school psychologist, a judge ruled that Yaeli could receive cross-sex hormones." (First Liberty brief, p. 3.) ADF's version: "After a recommendation from the school psychologist, a judge ruled that Yaeli could receive cross-sex hormones." — [ADF amicus for Martinez, *Heaps v. Delaware Valley Reg. H.S.*, 3d Cir. No. 24-3278, 2025-07-07](https://adflegal.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/heaps-v-delaware-valley-rhs-boe-2025-07-07-appellate-amicus-abigail-martinez.pdf). Prescriber not identified in the sources read.
- *Death:* suicide in September 2019 at age 19 (an adult, after leaving foster care). — [same First Liberty brief, pp. 1, 3–4](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-840/396397/20260217122626997_25-840%20Brief.pdf)
- *Responses:* District: "a claim suggesting our school or a staff member did not properly treat a student's severe depression is both completely inaccurate and troubling as our schools and staff would not be authorized or medically qualified to treat clinical depression" (quoted in the mother's brief, p. 4). DCFS reportedly declined comment on pending litigation (search-result summary of Epoch Times/New American coverage); the mother's brief says that in the civil suit DCFS "admitted that they 'aggressively pursued the implementation of inclusive, gender-affirming laws, policies, and supportive services for LGBTQ+ youth'" (her characterization of a litigation filing I did not see). — [First Liberty brief](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-840/396397/20260217122626997_25-840%20Brief.pdf); [The New American](https://thenewamerican.com/us/culture/grieving-california-mother-blames-school-district-l-a-county-and-transgender-advocates-for-her-daughters-death/)
- *Litigation:* *Estate of Yaeli Mozzelle Galdamez v. County of Los Angeles*, L.A. Superior Court No. 20STCV33881, filed 2020-09-04 against the County (DCFS), Pomona Valley Hospital and physicians; removed to federal court and reportedly remanded. I found no merits ruling, verdict or settlement; current status **unresolved** (docket listings only; opinion page unreadable). — [UniCourt listing](https://unicourt.com/case/ca-la23-the-estate-of-yaeli-mozzelle-galdamez-et-al-vs-county-of-los-angeles-by-and-through-department-of-children-and-family-services-et-al-586613); [CourtListener listing](https://www.courtlistener.com/opinion/9512300/the-estate-of-yaeli-mozzelle-galdamez-v-county-of-los-angeles/)
- *Label:* **reported allegation** (mother's unsworn account; court authorization and DCFS custody are asserted by the mother herself and not contradicted by any source found, but the underlying orders were not seen).
- *Parental involvement:* **a parent says she did not consent** / **conduct continued after explicit parental objection** / **a court or agency authorized the action** (mother's own account). The mother's consent was displaced because the teen was a juvenile-court dependent in DCFS custody.
- *Elements:* (1) school facilitation — alleged advocacy/recommendation by a school psychologist, not arrangement of treatment; unverified; (2) medication — testosterone reported (mother's account), prescriber unknown; (3) parent objected — yes per mother (she was aware, not unaware); (4) no court order — **fails**: a judge authorized hormones per the mother.

**B3. Kutzko family (Algonquin Regional H.S., Northborough-Southborough, Massachusetts; 2024–26) — testosterone sought by state agency, blocked by court**
- *School's documented/alleged role:* Parents and their attorney (Vernadette Broyles, Child & Parental Rights Campaign) allege "secret counseling sessions" in late 2024 that "endors[ed] the child's desire to be treated as something other than her sex," and that a counselor reported them to DCF. Court documents (per NBC Boston/WBUR/PinkNews) show the teen went to a school counselor the day after an alleged January 2025 assault and the school contacted police. No source alleges the school referred to a gender clinic or arranged medication. — [Boston.com, 2026-09-18](https://www.boston.com/news/local-news/2026/09/18/feds-investigating-mass-custody-case-involving-transgender-student-dcf/); [CBS Boston, 2026-09-17](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/trump-administration-southboro-northboro-school-student-gender-transition/); [PinkNews, 2026-09-18](https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/09/18/maura-healey-trans-custody-2026/); [NBC Boston, 2026-10-29](https://www.nbcboston.com/news/local/northborough-ma-court-hearing-gender-transition-kutzko-dcf/4039664/)
- *Why DCF has custody (contested):* Gov. Healey: "DCF did not remove the child because of a health issue or a gender-affirming care issue… DCF removed the child after there were criminal charges filed against the parents." Police statement of facts alleged the father "punched the victim with a closed fist in the shoulder area," pulled hair and made threats; mother later charged with witness intimidation. Father received pretrial probation without plea/finding (charges to be dismissed on conditions); DCF's February 2025 letter reportedly found abuse allegations "not supported" but supported neglect; a restraining order sought by the teen became permanent. Parents deny the allegations and say no court has found them unfit. — [Boston.com](https://www.boston.com/news/local-news/2026/09/18/feds-investigating-mass-custody-case-involving-transgender-student-dcf/); [WCVB, 2026-09-15](https://www.wcvb.com/article/massachusetts-dcf-gender-care-custody-case/73747124); [CBS Boston](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/trump-administration-southboro-northboro-school-student-gender-transition/)
- *Treatment:* The family's attorney says DCF took the position that high-dose testosterone is "ordinary care" and that "The court is not going to allow giving testosterone"; a Worcester Juvenile Court judge temporarily blocked/stayed DCF from giving testosterone (one outlet dates the order 2026-09-11). No source read establishes that testosterone was ever prescribed or administered; PinkNews (citing WBUR): "That does not establish that the teenager is receiving testosterone or other gender-affirming medication"; unclear whether the teen is following a doctor's recommendation. The attorney separately alleges DCF put the teen on birth control and psychiatric medication. — [WCVB](https://www.wcvb.com/article/massachusetts-dcf-gender-care-custody-case/73747124); [Voz, 2026-09-18](https://voz.us/en/society/260918/39224/catholic-parents-take-on-massachusetts-over-custody-of-their-daughter-after-accusing-school-of-initiating-transition-without-their-consent.html); [PinkNews](https://www.thepinknews.com/2026/09/18/maura-healey-trans-custody-2026/); [CBS Boston](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/trump-administration-southboro-northboro-school-student-gender-transition/). Note one outlet's looser paraphrase that "the state has administered gender-affirming care against the parents' wishes since the daughter was 15" (attorney's claim; type of care unspecified). — [Deseret News, 2026-09-16](https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/09/16/parents-fight-for-custody-of-children-who-want-gender-transition-with-states/)
- *Legal authority context:* 110 CMR 11.17(2): "The Department shall not give its consent to extraordinary medical treatment for any child in the care or custody of the Department. For all such children, the Department shall seek prior judicial approval for any extraordinary medical treatment (unless parental consent is obtained…)." Whether testosterone is "extraordinary" was the reported dispute. — [110 CMR 11.17 (Cornell LII)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/110-CMR-11-17)
- *Responses:* Superintendent Gregory Martineau: the district "unequivocally denies" the claims, calling them "not merely baseless but grossly insensitive, intrusive and dangerous," cites mandated-reporter duties, and "welcomes and will cooperate fully" with the federal probe. DCF declined comment citing privacy. — [Boston.com](https://www.boston.com/news/local-news/2026/09/18/feds-investigating-mass-custody-case-involving-transgender-student-dcf/)
- *Federal action:* ED Student Privacy Policy Office opened a FERPA/PPRA investigation of the district on 2026-09-17 (allegation: social transition/counseling without parental knowledge). No findings reported. — [Education Week, 2026-09-17](https://www.edweek.org/policy-politics/trump-admin-turns-to-student-privacy-laws-in-gender-identity-fights-with-schools/2026/09)
- *Status (checked 2026-11-18):* hearing held 2026-10-29 in Worcester Juvenile Court; no ruling reported; full trial delayed to March 2027; teen turns 18 in April 2027; earlier testosterone block reported as still in place. — [Epoch Times, 2026-11-09](https://www.theepochtimes.com/article/parents-locked-in-battle-with-state-over-daughters-custody-transgender-treatment-6111344)
- *Label:* school "secret counseling" = **reported allegation** (denied by district); mandated report and criminal charges = **established by records** (court documents as described by multiple outlets); DCF seeking testosterone and the court's block = **reported allegation / corroborated in part** (single origin — the family's attorney — repeated by several outlets; the juvenile file is sealed); testosterone actually given = **unresolved** (no evidence found).
- *Parental involvement:* **conduct continued after explicit parental objection** (as to social/psychiatric care, alleged); for testosterone: **a court … [declined to] authorize the action** so far; parents **notified and objecting**. Parents retain legal rights (not terminated) but the teen is in DCF custody.
- *Elements:* (1) school facilitation of medication — **not alleged** (school role = counseling + abuse report); (2) medication administered — **unproven**, reportedly blocked; (3) parents unaware — **fails** (they know and object); (4) no court order — court is actively supervising.

**B4. *Doe v. Weiser* / School District 27J (Brighton H.S., Colorado) — hormones/mastectomy allegedly *discussed* by a therapist reached through the school; no treatment**
- Complaint (filed 2024-08-07; Center for American Liberty) alleges a school counselor connected the 14-year-old with a therapist via Colorado's I Matter tele-therapy portal, using the counselor's computer; the therapist allegedly discussed/recommended hormone treatment and mastectomy without the parents' knowledge; the school used a male name at school and the female name with parents. The daughter later desisted; no prescription or surgery is alleged. — [CNA/National Catholic Register, 2024-08-14](https://www.ncregister.com/cna/parents-sue-colorado-school-for-secretly-facilitating-daughter-s-gender-transition)
- CNN (2026): the parents "would not consent," and the daughter resumed identifying as a girl; no surgery. — [CNN via KEYT, 2026-10-20](https://keyt.com/politics/cnn-us-politics/2026/10/20/fact-check-trump-brings-back-his-2024-lie-that-schools-are-secretly-sending-kids-to-hospitals-for-gender-surgery/)
- Ruling: U.S. District Judge Charlotte Sweeney denied a preliminary injunction (no ongoing/imminent injury shown) and dismissed the Attorney General as a defendant, 2025-01-27; other defendants' motions to dismiss were pending then. Later status not found (**unresolved**). — [Colorado Politics, 2025-01-27](https://www.coloradopolitics.com/2025/01/27/federal-judge-rejects-challenge-to-colorados-chosen-name-law-district-27js-lgbtq-policy-85c540e5-56b6-5a0f-8709-6e427b37086d/)
- *Label:* **reported allegation** (complaint; defendants' response not found). *Parental involvement:* **parents not notified** of counseling/social transition (alleged); parents later refused consent to any medical step. *Elements:* (1) referral to therapy — alleged; (2) treatment — **fails** (discussion only); (3) alleged; (4) n/a.

**B5. Holman family (Puyallup H.S., Washington) — counselor allegedly gave brochures/list of gender clinics; no medication documented**
- Parents' amicus brief: the school's mental-health counselor gave the student "brochures for transgender support clinics and encourag[ed] her to visit during school hours, without telling her parents"; when confronted "the counselor insisted that she had every right to provide the transgender resources." The teen was later connected with TeamChild (youth legal aid); a court granted an Emergency Minor Guardianship with a third party on/after 2025-04-24 and a commissioner declined to extend it on 2025-05-21, returning her to the parents; family moved to Texas; teen ran away 2025-11-28. The brief does not allege that hormones/blockers were prescribed or obtained. — [Brief of Jodie and David Holman, Ashly Wallace et al., No. 25-840, filed 2026-02-13, pp. 10–15](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-840/396272/20260213155020751_Brief%20of%20Amici%20Curiae%20Supporting%20Petitioners.pdf)
- Media version: mother found "a list of gender care facilities" in the backpack; article "does not say whether hormones, puberty blockers, or surgery were prescribed or obtained"; no district response included. — [Seattle Red, 2026-07-24](https://seattlered.com/seattle-red/wa-school-gender-care/4119542)
- *Label:* **reported allegation** (parents' unsworn account; one origin). *Parental involvement:* **parents not notified** (of the referral information); later **conduct continued after explicit parental objection** (social affirmation). *Elements:* (1) information/referral list — alleged; (2) treatment — **none shown**; a referral alone does not establish treatment.

**B6. Ashly Wallace / "Wynter" (Oregon) — mother *believes* daughter received testosterone as a minor; no school link to the medication**
- Amicus account: the school enrolled the student in a trans support club and arranged/invited "confidential affirming therapy" without the mother's knowledge; at 15 she ran away, used shelters and ODHS processes; the mother "has not heard from Wynter in over a year, and believes she did not finish high school and was placed on testosterone as a minor without parental consent." No provider, date or record is cited, and the school is not alleged to have arranged the testosterone. — [Holman/Wallace cert-stage brief, pp. 15–18](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-840/396272/20260213155020751_Brief%20of%20Amici%20Curiae%20Supporting%20Petitioners.pdf); [Our Duty-USA et al. merits-stage brief, filed 2026-10-12, pp. 32–33](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-840/429216/20261012130311539_25-840%20Amicus%20Brief%20Merits%20Stage.pdf)
- *Law:* Oregon lets a minor 15 or older consent, "without the consent of a parent or guardian," to medical diagnosis or treatment by a physician, PA or NP (ORS 109.640(4)); the provider *may* tell the parent without the minor's consent (ORS 109.650). — [ORS ch. 109](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors109.html)
- *Label:* **reported allegation** (a parent's belief). *Parental involvement:* **applicable law allowed the minor to consent independently** (if treatment occurred at 15+); otherwise **unknown or disputed**. *Elements:* (1) school facilitation of medication — **not alleged**; (2) medication — **unproven**; (3) mother not consenting — asserted; (4) no court order — none needed under ORS 109.640 at 15+.

**B7. Kaya Clementine Breen (California) — school counselor told the parents; parents consented**
- Detransitioner amicus: at ~11 she told a school counselor life would be easier as a boy; "the counselor concluded that Clementine was transgender and informed her parents"; the parents then sought a gender clinic; blocker implant 2017-03-06 (age 12), testosterone January 2018 (age 13) after the parents — who "opposed the treatment" — "consented" when the physician allegedly told them she was suicidal; mastectomy May 2019 (age 14), mother signing consent. — [Brief of Detransitioners, No. 25-840, filed 2026-09-15, pp. 12–14](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-840/424397/20260915173455112_25-840%20Amicus%20Brief.pdf)
- *Label:* **sworn but contested allegation**/complaint allegation in pending malpractice litigation against providers (not against the school). *Parental involvement:* parents notified by the school and **consented** (they allege consent was obtained by misrepresentation). *Elements:* (1) school role = disclosure to parents; (3) **fails** — parents knew and consented.

## Key Question 3: What explanations do the records support for the scarcity of qualifying cases? (evidence-backed observations, not a conclusion that none exists)

### Takeaway
The records point to structural consent gates: in most states minors cannot consent to blockers/hormones, providers and SBHC operators state written parental-consent requirements, child-welfare agencies need court approval for extraordinary treatment, and schools/school nurses have neither the drugs nor the authority — while the two notable statutory exceptions (Oregon at 15+, Maine at 16+ under conditions) run through licensed clinicians, not school staff. These observations explain why facilitation claims cluster around social transition and referrals rather than treatment; they do not prove no exception has occurred.

### Cited Findings
- **Parental consent is the stated default for minors' gender-related medication.** CNN, citing clinicians: even where legal for minors, parental consent is required for blockers/hormones; PolitiFact: AAP, WPATH and Endocrine Society standards require parental consent/involvement; schools "generally do not provide students with medical care without parental consent." — [CNN via KEYT](https://keyt.com/politics/cnn-us-politics/2026/10/20/fact-check-trump-brings-back-his-2024-lie-that-schools-are-secretly-sending-kids-to-hospitals-for-gender-surgery/); [PolitiFact](https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2024/sep/05/donald-trump/trumps-pants-on-fire-claim-that-schools-are-perfor)
- **School nurses' scope:** NASN leaders say school nurses treat acute episodic illness/injury; gender surgery "Not ever"; "even giving a student Tylenol typically requires written parent or guardian permission." — [PolitiFact](https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2024/sep/05/donald-trump/trumps-pants-on-fire-claim-that-schools-are-perfor); [CNN via KEYT](https://keyt.com/politics/cnn-us-politics/2026/10/20/fact-check-trump-brings-back-his-2024-lie-that-schools-are-secretly-sending-kids-to-hospitals-for-gender-surgery/)
- **Surgery on minors is rare and parent-mediated:** a JAMA Network Open analysis of 2019 claims (22.8 million insured minors) found 85 gender-affirming surgeries, 82 of them chest procedures. — [PolitiFact](https://www.politifact.com/factchecks/2024/sep/05/donald-trump/trumps-pants-on-fire-claim-that-schools-are-perfor)
- **Washington:** the State's Supreme Court brief says RCW 71.34.530 (age 13+, outpatient behavioral health) "does not permit minors to consent to medical care like puberty blockers or hormone replacement therapy," and that the challenged runaway-youth laws authorize only voluntary referrals for behavioral health services; providers may disclose minors' mental-health records to parents (RCW 70.02.240; 71.34.430). — [Brief for Respondents, No. 25-840, 2026-10-30](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-840/431251/20261030103650623_BrRespts_Corrected.pdf). Advocacy guidance asserts a common-law "mature minor" route under which a provider may deem a minor able to consent — a clinician-by-clinician determination, not a school function (search-level summary; primary text not read). — [NW Justice Project guidance (hosted by NWESD)](https://www.nwesd.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Section-10-NW-Justice-Project-5934en_minor-healthcare-no-parental-consent-guidance.pdf)
- **Washington SBHC operators' written positions:** Neighborcare — "This level of care can only be consented to by a parent or legal guardian"; Country Doctor — "seeking and receiving the consent of parents or legal guardians." Seattle Children's gender clinic page (search summary) likewise requires parent/guardian consent unless the minor is emancipated. — [Defending Education](https://defendinged.org/incidents/seattle-public-schools-school-based-health-center-provider-offers-students-gender-affirming-care-services-including-hormonal-treatment-emails-reveal/); [KOMO](https://komonews.com/news/local/gender-affirming-care-seattle-students-lgbt-trans-transgender-health-center-washington-on-campus-services-sps-gay-academic-transition-non-profit-parents-law-regulation-doctor-community); [Seattle Children's Gender Clinic](https://www.seattlechildrens.org/clinics/gender-clinic/)
- **Oregon (exception):** ORS 109.640(4) — "A minor 15 years of age or older may give consent, without the consent of a parent or guardian" to medical or surgical diagnosis or treatment by a physician, and diagnosis/treatment by a PA or NP; ORS 109.650 permits (does not require) the provider to advise the parent without the minor's consent. Oregon DOJ states minors 15+ can consent to gender-affirming treatment (search summary). So in Oregon a 15–17-year-old's hormones without parental consent would be lawful minor consent, not an unauthorized act, and no court order would be involved. — [ORS ch. 109](https://www.oregonlegislature.gov/bills_laws/ors/ors109.html); [Oregon DOJ resources page](https://www.doj.state.or.us/oregon-department-of-justice/civil-rights/gender-diverse-community-resources/)
- **Maine (conditional exception):** 22 MRSA §1508 (PL 2023, c. 413; amended PL 2025, c. 738) lets a licensed physician/APRN provide gender-affirming hormone therapy to a minor without parental consent only if the minor is at least 16, has a gender-dysphoria diagnosis, is (or is expected to be) experiencing harm without it, reports having discussed it with a parent/guardian who "refused to support treatment," and gives informed written consent kept in the health record. The definition includes interventions "to suppress the development of endogenous secondary sex characteristics." By design the parent has been told of the dysphoria but need not be told of the treatment. — [22 MRSA §1508](https://legislature.maine.gov/statutes/22/title22sec1508.html)
- **Maine SBHCs:** an advocacy forum (2025-11-20) asserted a "pipeline" via SBHCs and named MaineHealth as providing gender-affirming treatment, but described no specific child, clinic, school or date and included no operator response; in an earlier Gardiner dispute the SBHC sponsor said its centers comply with state law and the superintendent reportedly said the center will not prescribe hormone blockers (search summaries). — [Maine Wire, 2025-11-21](https://www.themainewire.com/2025/11/maine-experts-warn-school-based-health-centers-lead-to-gender-care-without-parental-consent/); [Daily Signal, 2024-12-09](https://www.dailysignal.com/2024/12/09/parents-outraged-maine-school-clinic-could-give-students-confidential-reproductive-health-care/)
- **Child-welfare custody runs through courts:** Massachusetts 110 CMR 11.17(2) bars DCF from consenting to extraordinary medical treatment and requires prior judicial approval absent parental consent; in the Kutzko matter a juvenile court reportedly blocked testosterone. California AB 2119 (2018) recognizes gender-affirming care as part of foster youths' health-care rights but "subject to existing laws governing consent to health care for minors" (search summary of bill text); in Yaeli's 2016–17 case a judge reportedly made the hormone decision. — [110 CMR 11.17](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/massachusetts/110-CMR-11-17); [WCVB](https://www.wcvb.com/article/massachusetts-dcf-gender-care-custody-case/73747124); [AB 2119 text (LegiScan)](https://legiscan.com/CA/text/AB2119/id/1820093); [First Liberty brief, p. 3](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-840/396397/20260217122626997_25-840%20Brief.pdf)
- **Hospitals asking parents:** even in a complaint hostile to Washington's laws, the hospital "asked permission to put her on puberty blockers, but [the parents] declined," and treatment did not proceed. — [IPEC cert petition appendix 77a](https://www.supremecourt.gov/DocketPDF/25/25-840/391642/20260113192137323_Petition%20for%20Cert%20w%20Appx%201.13.26.pdf)
- **Non-school community/telehealth providers:** a syndicated national report says Planned Parenthood requires parental consent to begin hormones under 18, with several affiliates limiting hormone therapy to 18+ (affiliate rules vary; not verified against each affiliate); QueerDoc says it requires parent/guardian consent for youth unless a state's mature-minor doctrine applies; Plume describes its members as adults (search summaries). No source found alleges school staff helped a minor use these services. — [The National Desk via ABC News 4](https://abcnews4.com/news/nation-world/planned-parenthood-giving-transgender-hormone-therapy-to-minors-across-the-country); [QueerDoc](https://queerdoc.com/?p=11122); [Plume support](https://support.getplume.co/support/solutions/articles/72000545369-how-plume-works)

### Inferences
- The consent architecture places the prescribing decision with licensed clinicians who must document consent (parent, statutory minor consent, or court), so a school employee has no lawful means to "arrange" medication unilaterally; the realistic school touchpoints are counseling, information/referral, abuse reporting, and (rarely alleged) handing over non-prescription items such as binders — which matches the distribution of allegations actually found.
- Where a minor-consent statute applies (Oregon 15+, Maine 16+ conditions), a parent's lack of consent would not make the treatment unauthorized, which may also reduce litigation and public records of such events — so scarcity of documented cases in those states is weaker evidence of non-occurrence than in parental-consent states such as California and Washington.
- Insurance explanation-of-benefits statements and pharmacy records plausibly make sustained undisclosed hormone treatment of a minor on a parent's policy hard to conceal; I found no source directly documenting this mechanism for gender-related care, so this remains an inference rather than a cited finding.
- Given intense advocacy, litigation and federal investigative attention in 2024–2026 (White House, ED, DOJ, dozens of amici), the failure of motivated parties to produce a qualifying case when directly asked (CNN 2024 and 2026) is meaningful but not conclusive evidence of rarity.
