# Orange County, Pasadena Unified and Santa Clarita "Lydia": minors, gender-related school actions, and parental knowledge/consent

Research date: 2026-11-18. "Status checked through" dates give the date of the latest source I could actually retrieve, not today's date.

Evidence labels used: **[records]** established by records; **[corroborated]** corroborated account; **[sworn/contested]** sworn but contested allegation; **[reported]** reported allegation; **[unresolved]**.

Four-element test (applied to every incident): (1) school/employee arranged or materially facilitated; (2) gender-related medication or surgery actually prescribed/dispensed/administered; (3) relevant parent unaware or had not consented; (4) no prior court order authorized it.

**Bottom line across the whole scope: no incident found satisfies element (2). Every documented matter is social transition and/or information withholding. No source alleges that a school employee arranged an appointment, transport, prescription or procedure.**

## 1. Newport-Mesa Unified (Newport Beach / Costa Mesa): individual incidents, policy, actors

### Takeaway
No lawsuit, tort claim or named-family incident was found for NMUSD. What exists is (a) an advocacy group's 2023 reading of district emails claiming 23 students got "gender support plans" without informed parental consent, which the district denied, and (b) district documents showing parental involvement in such plans is not required for students 12 and older. No medical treatment or staff-arranged medical referral is documented; the only medical-adjacent claims concern web links and QR codes to outside resources, which the district disputes.

### Cited Findings

**(a) Individual-incident level (none identifiable)**
- **[reported]** California Alliance for Education (parent group) obtained "hundreds of pages" of NMUSD emails by records request and claimed 23 students (8 elementary) began social transition "without informed parental consent" from Jan. 2020 to Oct. 2022. The spokesperson used a pseudonym ("Kim Davis") and conceded "some of the parents of the 23 children may have been informed later." — [California Insider/Epoch Times, 2023-03-18, upd. 2023-05-05](https://californiainsider.com/news/california-school-district-emails-reveal-students-were-secretly-gender-transitioned-5131093)
- **[reported]** The same article lists email-derived episodes without names, schools or dates: "a mom didn't want her child to transition, and the school moved forward with the social transition of the child anyway"; a high-school student's parents did not attend a gender-support meeting "because the student didn't want the parents to know"; a principal told a counselor to "build connection" with an elementary student and ask about pronouns. The Epoch Times says it obtained the emails; the emails themselves are not published in the article. — [California Insider, 2023-03-18](https://californiainsider.com/news/california-school-district-emails-reveal-students-were-secretly-gender-transitioned-5131093)
- District response: spokesperson Annette Franco said the district "wasn't aware of the 23 cases" and that it must keep gender support plans confidential unless students permit disclosure. — [California Insider, 2023-03-18](https://californiainsider.com/news/california-school-district-emails-reveal-students-were-secretly-gender-transitioned-5131093)
- District response at the Mar. 28, 2023 board meeting: Assistant Supt. Sara Jocham said GSPs "are not transition plans" and the district provides no medical support; Assistant Supt. Kerrie Torres said the emails include a "conversation that does look scary," suggested possible "staff overreach," and said a third party was investigating staff participation in GSPs. Franco on Mar. 30 denied any "secret social gender transitions." — [Epoch Times, 2023-03-31, upd. 2023-04-07](https://www.theepochtimes.com/us/school-district-officials-deny-secret-social-gender-transitions-board-members-request-investigation-5163598)
- Trustees Michelle Barto, Krista Weigand and Lisa Pearson called the allegations "sickening and appalling" (Mar. 19, 2023 statement), asked Supt. Wesley Smith for a review, and said they would push for a parental-transparency policy. — [Epoch Times, 2023-03-31](https://www.theepochtimes.com/us/school-district-officials-deny-secret-social-gender-transitions-board-members-request-investigation-5163598)
- Parental-involvement category for the 23 claimed cases: **unknown or disputed** (group says "not informed, or informed and didn't consent"; district denies). One email-derived case is described as **conduct continued after explicit parental objection**, but it is unverified and unidentifiable.
- Four-element test (23-case claim): (1) alleged, disputed; (2) **not alleged**; (3) alleged, disputed; (4) not applicable. **Test not met.**

**(b) Policy / regulation / documents**
- **[records]** NMUSD Regulation 5145.3 (Nondiscrimination/Harassment) says a student's transgender status "is his/her private information and the district will only disclose the information to others with the student's prior written consent," except where required by law or where there is "compelling evidence that disclosure is necessary to preserve the student's physical or mental well-being." The compliance officer meets the student "and, if appropriate, his/her parents/guardians." Quoted with a link to the regulation PDF. — [Defending Education, 2023-03-28](https://defendinged.org/incidents/newport-mesa-unified-school-district-has-policy-to-keep-parents-in-the-dark-regarding-their-childrens-gender-identity/)
- **[reported, document-based]** A "Student Gender Support Plan Process" document (last updated Sept. 2023; obtained by an anonymous resident's CPRA request) makes parental involvement mandatory only for ages 11 and under; even then, if the child does not want a parent involved the district will "proceed to support the student." — [Daily Signal, 2024-08-13 (Wayback copy read)](https://www.dailysignal.com/2024/08/13/california-district-allows-12-year-olds-hide-gender-identity-parents-denies-secretly-transitioning-kids/)
- **[reported, document-based]** Aug. 7, 2022 email from Student Services administrative director Sarah Coley (obtained by Center for American Liberty): "It is encouraged to have parents part of the process, but under the law, it is not required." — [Daily Signal, 2024-08-13](https://www.dailysignal.com/2024/08/13/california-district-allows-12-year-olds-hide-gender-identity-parents-denies-secretly-transitioning-kids/)
- District response (Newport Harbor High message to families, Aug. 9, 2024): "We do not refer students for gender-affirming care, nor do we have gender support plans without parental involvement"; "none of our direct links are connected to gender transition counseling/therapy." — [Daily Signal, 2024-08-13](https://www.dailysignal.com/2024/08/13/california-district-allows-12-year-olds-hide-gender-identity-parents-denies-secretly-transitioning-kids/)
- The advocacy group dates the regulation to a 2019 board adoption and calls it a "Parental Secrecy Policy" (their characterization). — [School Board Watchlist, undated](https://www.schoolboardwatchlist.org/districts/newportmesaunifiedschooldistrict)

**(c) Medical-adjacent claims (information/referral only, disputed)**
- **[reported]** Parent Haley Jenkins said Newport Harbor High's website linked to "lawyers, doctors, and surgeons" for gender-affirming care and, "until a few months ago," to a UC Irvine surgeons' meet-and-greet and sources for chest binders. No student is alleged to have used them. — [California Insider, 2023-03-18](https://californiainsider.com/news/california-school-district-emails-reveal-students-were-secretly-gender-transitioned-5131093)
- **[reported, disputed]** Daily Signal said hallway QR codes at Newport Harbor High led to the LGBTQ Center OC "LGBTQ Affirming Therapy" page, which had a form to connect with a therapist who can write referral letters. The superintendent denied this at the July 30, 2024 board meeting. — [Daily Signal, 2024-08-13](https://www.dailysignal.com/2024/08/13/california-district-allows-12-year-olds-hide-gender-identity-parents-denies-secretly-transitioning-kids/); [Daily Signal, 2024-08-05](https://www.dailysignal.com/2024/08/05/superintendent-denies-district-linked-lgbtq-resources-taken-day-daily-signal-report/)

**(d) Actors and documented relationships**
- California Alliance for Education: records requester and publicist of the 23-case claim. Center for American Liberty: obtained internal emails by records request. — [California Insider, 2023-03-18](https://californiainsider.com/news/california-school-district-emails-reveal-students-were-secretly-gender-transitioned-5131093); [Daily Signal, 2024-08-13](https://www.dailysignal.com/2024/08/13/california-district-allows-12-year-olds-hide-gender-identity-parents-denies-secretly-transitioning-kids/)
- LAist (2025) described NMUSD as having "largely stayed out of" the parental-notification fights. Barto left the board after winning a Newport Beach council seat; appointee Kirstin Walsh (who wrote that she agreed with AB 1955) faced Andrea McElroy in a June 10, 2025 special election. — [LAist, 2025](https://laist.com/news/politics/voter-game-plan-guide-to-the-newport-mesa-unified-school-board-special-election)

### Inferences
- The 2023 "23 students" figure is a single-source advocacy reading of emails. Epoch Times, California Insider, NTD and RedState repeat one account, so they are not independent corroboration.
- The district's 2024 statement ("no gender support plans without parental involvement") is in tension with its own Sept. 2023 process document and the Coley email. That is a policy-level contradiction, not proof of any specific concealed case.

### Gaps
- Outcome of the 2023 third-party investigation: not found (searched Epoch Times, Daily Pilot, district terms).
- No NMUSD board vote on a parental-notification policy found; no NMUSD lawsuit or claim found on CourtListener-indexed sources or in news searches. OC Superior Court's case index was not searched directly.
- The underlying emails and the full Reg. 5145.3 PDF were not retrieved directly. Result of the June 10, 2025 special election not verified.
- Status checked through: 2025 (LAist election guide); no 2026 NMUSD-specific development found.

## 2. Irvine Unified: individual incidents, policy, actors

### Takeaway
No lawsuit, tort claim or identifiable individual incident was found for IUSD. The record is policy-level: internal gender-support-plan guidance obtained by records request, a count of 110 plans, and the district's January 2024 refusal to agendize a parent-drafted notification policy. No medical referral or treatment by school staff is alleged.

### Cited Findings
- **[reported, document-based]** Documents "uncovered by the Center for American Liberty and shared with The Federalist" include "Gender Support Plan Procedures & Best Practice Guidelines," "Gender Affirming School Culture," and "Gender Support Advocate Considerations." Quoted lines: staff "may not share a student's gender identity with the student's parents" (marked internal use only); "You must not reveal any information about the student that could be seen as a violation of the child's privacy rights." No individual student is described; IUSD did not respond to the outlet; no medical referral is mentioned. — [The Federalist, 2025-07-29](https://thefederalist.com/2025/07/29/docs-irvine-schools-coach-teachers-on-transitioning-kids-against-challenging-parents-wishes/)
- **[reported]** A CPRA response reportedly showed 110 gender support plans (14 elementary); staff guidance reportedly says to "continue to use [normal pronouns] in communication with the family" and to "not mention a gender support plan." Advocacy outlet; underlying records not published in the article. — [SoCal Daily Pulse, undated](https://socaldailypulse.com/post/irvine-unified-implements-disturbing-policy-of-lying-to-parents-about-secretive-gender-support-plans/)
- **[reported]** Parent group "Informed Parents of IUSD" says records obtained in summer 2023 showed plans rose from 23 to 110 in under a year. Parent Erin Wozneak asked on Jan. 12, 2024 to agendize a notification policy modeled on Orange Unified's; the district declined by email on Jan. 17, 2024, citing "a great deal of uncertainty regarding the legal authorities." — [Tiffany Craft Substack, 2024-01-28](https://tiffanycraft.substack.com/p/irvine-unified-denies-parental-notification)
- District-side statement at the Jan. 23, 2024 meeting: IUSD LGBTQ+ Community Support Liaison Maureen Muir called the plan a "life saving document" and said "Any and all changes to name and gender fields in Aeries are immediately seen in parent portal." — [Tiffany Craft Substack, 2024-01-28](https://tiffanycraft.substack.com/p/irvine-unified-denies-parental-notification)
- **[reported, second-hand]** At that meeting Brenda Lebsack (Santa Ana Unified teacher, former Orange Unified trustee) recounted that in 2019 a mother told her an autistic high-school son was socially transitioned at school without the parents' knowledge and moved out at 18 with an activist group's help. District and school are not identified; no records cited. — [California Insider, 2024-01-25](https://californiainsider.com/california-news/education-family/community-members-advocate-for-parent-notification-policy-at-irvine-unified-5573999)
- Parental-involvement category (IUSD generally): **unknown or disputed**; policy permits non-notification, but no case is documented.
- Four-element test: no incident to test. For the Lebsack anecdote: (1) alleged; (2) **not alleged**; (3) alleged; (4) n/a. **Not met.**
- Actors: Center for American Liberty (records requests; CEO Mark Trammell quoted); Informed Parents of IUSD (policy drafting); OC Board of Education trustee Mari Barke and Lebsack (public comment); two student board members and three clergy opposed. — [The Federalist, 2025-07-29](https://thefederalist.com/2025/07/29/docs-irvine-schools-coach-teachers-on-transitioning-kids-against-challenging-parents-wishes/); [Tiffany Craft Substack, 2024-01-28](https://tiffanycraft.substack.com/p/irvine-unified-denies-parental-notification)

### Inferences
- Muir's statement that Aeries name/gender changes show in the parent portal and the advocacy claim that staff are told not to mention plans to families can both be true if plans exist without an Aeries change. Neither was checked against the records.

### Gaps
- IUSD's AR 5145.3 text and the actual guidance documents were not retrieved. No IUSD statement responding to The Federalist found.
- No Center for American Liberty v. IUSD records suit found. No C.D. Cal. or reported OC Superior Court case found.
- Status checked through: 2025-07-29 (The Federalist).

## 3. Rest of Orange County: Orange Unified, Capistrano, Placentia-Yorba Linda, OC Board of Education, Huntington Beach, court cases, medical providers

### Takeaway
The only identifiable Orange County family incident found is pseudonymous "Parent 7A / child 7C" in *City of Huntington Beach v. Newsom*: a complaint allegation of school-side name change, unconsented counseling and a CPS report against the mother, in an unnamed OC district, with no medical element. Everything else is policy: Orange Unified adopted a notification policy (Sept. 2023), two sponsors were recalled (Mar. 2024), and the policy was rescinded (Nov. 2024). No OC medical-provider incident of treatment without parental consent was found.

### Cited Findings

**(a) Individual incident: "Family 7" (unnamed Orange County district)**
- **[complaint allegation, unsworn pleading; later a declaration exists on the docket]** 7A is the mother of a 16-year-old girl attending "public school in Orange County," in the only district she had attended since kindergarten. 7A first learned at 8th-grade graduation that the school used a male name. In high school the counselor refused to enter the legal name in the system for fear the student would be "outed." The counselor "would regularly pull [7C] out of class to have counseling sessions with her" without 7A's permission or knowledge. After 7A objected by email, the counselor reported her to CPS for "emotional abuse"; CPS "found nothing amiss and took no action." — [Complaint ¶¶107–126, filed 2024-09-17 (orig. No. 2:24-cv-07959)](https://legalinsurrection.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/City-of-Huntington-Beach-v.-Newsom.pdf)
- Who did it: unnamed high-school counselor and school staff. No district, school or employee is named, and the district is not a party, so there is no district response.
- Parental involvement: **parents not notified** at the outset; then **conduct continued after explicit parental objection** (7A's emailed request to use the legal name). Father's position not stated. Whose consent matters: 7A as custodial parent of a minor.
- Medical: **the complaint does not allege any referral, clinic, medication or surgery for 7C.**
- Four-element test: (1) social transition and counseling alleged; (2) **no**; (3) alleged; (4) n/a. **Not met.**
- Judicial treatment: the district court held 7A (with 1A, 4A, 5A, 8A) had standing but dismissed all claims under Rule 12(b)(6) without leave to amend, finding no fundamental right and that AB 1955 survives rational-basis review. This is a pleading-stage ruling, not a finding of fact. — [Order, ECF 96, 2025-06-16, pp. 12–14, 33–34](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/attachments/press-docs/ECF%2096%20Order%20on%20Motion%20to%20Dismiss.pdf)
- The same order notes only Parent 10A lives in Huntington Beach, and 10A alleges an autism diagnosis as risk, not any gender-identity event. — [Order, ECF 96, pp. 3, 10](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/attachments/press-docs/ECF%2096%20Order%20on%20Motion%20to%20Dismiss.pdf)

**(b) Huntington Beach ordinance and *City of Huntington Beach v. Newsom***
- The ordinance designates the city a "Parents' Right to Know" city and says no educators in the city "shall withhold any information related to a child's sexual orientation, gender identity, or gender expression to Parents"; it authorizes suit against AB 1955. — [Order, ECF 96, p. 3](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/attachments/press-docs/ECF%2096%20Order%20on%20Motion%20to%20Dismiss.pdf)

- Suit filed Sept. 17, 2024; First Amended Complaint Dec. 3, 2024; C.D. Cal. No. 8:24-cv-02017-CBM-JDE (Judge Consuelo Marshall). City dismissed for lack of standing. — [CourtListener docket](https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/69175158/city-of-huntington-beach-v-gavin-newsom/); [Order, ECF 96](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/attachments/press-docs/ECF%2096%20Order%20on%20Motion%20to%20Dismiss.pdf)

This is preliminary relief, not a merits judgment. — [Ninth Circuit order, 2026-06-18, p. 10](https://www.schaerr-jaffe.com/wp-content/uploads/CA9-decision-granting-PI-pending-appeal.pdf)

- Representation (documented): America First Legal (Nicholas Barry, Ian Prior), Schaerr Jaffe (Gene Schaerr et al.), HB City Attorney Michael Vigliotta (earlier Michael Gates). — [Schaerr Jaffe, 2026-06-23](https://www.schaerr-jaffe.com/ninth-circuit-grants-preliminary-injunction-pending-appeal-in-city-of-huntington-beach-v-newsom/); [AFL case page](https://aflegal.org/litigation/city-of-huntington-beach-v-gavin-newsom-et-al/)

## 4. Pasadena Unified: individual incident vs. policy vs. disputed reporting; dockets

### Takeaway
The Pasadena matter is the pseudonymous "John and Jane Doe" family in the federal class action *Mirabelli v. Olson / Mirabelli v. Bonta*. Their sworn account is that a PUSD secondary school used a male name for their daughter and would not tell them, citing state guidance; it involves no medical treatment, and the parents themselves arranged therapy. PUSD is not a defendant, and the identification of PUSD comes from the plaintiffs' August 2026 appellate brief, not from the complaint or the Supreme Court opinion.

### Cited Findings

**(a) Individual incident: Doe family**
- Complaint (pseudonymous; district not named): Child Doe, 14 in Aug. 2024, attended "an expanded secondary school (6-12) within a public school district in the County of Los Angeles"; the family is Roman Catholic. — [Second Amended Class Action Complaint ¶128, ECF 133, 2024-08-13](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.casd.757886/gov.uscourts.casd.757886.133.0.pdf)
- April 2021 (5th grade): a friend's mother told Mrs. Doe the child had announced a male name/pronouns to her daughter. Sept. 2021 (6th grade): the child gave her parents a note, "I am trans," asking for a haircut and chest binder; the parents told her they did not agree to social transition "at school—or anywhere." — [SAC ¶¶133, 139–140](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.casd.757886/gov.uscourts.casd.757886.133.0.pdf)
- 2022–23 (7th grade): Mrs. Doe emailed the principal; the reply (late Jan. 2023) was "We support our LGBTQ students and follow state Education Code" with a link to the CDE FAQ. A found note showed a classmate "deadnaming" the child "in front of the whole class." Three teachers, asked if there was anything the mother should know, each said "No." In Feb. 2023 the principal "denied any knowledge of the social transition and even denied that it was really happening," but said that if a child asked for secrecy, "We have to do that, it's the law." — [SAC ¶¶142–148](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.casd.757886/gov.uscourts.casd.757886.133.0.pdf)

## 5. Santa Clarita "Lydia" (William S. Hart Union HSD, Academy of the Canyons)

### Takeaway
"Lydia" is a real, self-identified mother whose account is partly corroborated by a district law-firm memo: the school used a male name for her daughter, she was not told, and the district's stated rule was non-disclosure without student consent. Her first public telling was a January 2023 Hart board comment; the fullest independent report is The 74 (June 9, 2025). No lawsuit was filed, no medical treatment occurred, and no school employee is alleged to have made a medical referral.

### Cited Findings

**(a) Origin and chain of the account**
- First public forum: a roughly two-minute comment at a Hart district board meeting in January 2023, later shared on social media; trustee Joe Messina then connected her with Pacific Justice Institute. — [The 74, 2025-06-09](https://www.the74million.org/article/the-california-mom-at-the-center-of-trumps-crackdown-on-school-gender-policies/)
- Her own Substack ("A Mother's thoughts") has posts dated from 2022-12-07; a long first-person audio account is dated 2024-07-25; a Nov. 15, 2026 post gives her current version (a parent-panel talk for SEGM). — [Substack archive](https://lydia833.substack.com/archive?sort=new); [2024-07-25 post](https://lydia833.substack.com/p/a-moms-journey-in-the-pit-of-gender); [2026-11-15 post](https://lydia833.substack.com/p/parent-panel-for-segm)
- Advocacy retelling: Erin Friday (Our Duty) as "Lydia M.," child "T." — [Our Duty, 2024-09-22](https://ourduty.group/2024/09/22/what-happens-when-schools-secretly-socially-transition-a-child/)
- Used as the lead example in attorney Julie Hamill's (California Justice Center) letter asking the U.S. Education Department to investigate California; the letter is dated "January 31, 2024" on its face but is addressed to January 2025 officials and reported as Jan. 31, 2025. — [Hamill letter](https://www.the74million.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/CaliforniaFERPAandTitleIXUSDOELTR1.31.25-1-1.pdf); [The 74, 2026-01-29](https://www.the74million.org/article/ed-dept-says-california-violated-law-by-concealing-students-gender-identity/)
- The 74's later article gives the mother's full name; these notes do not repeat it. — [The 74, 2026-01-29](https://www.the74million.org/article/ed-dept-says-california-violated-law-by-concealing-students-gender-identity/)

**(b) What is established by records**
- **[records]** AALRR memo to the Hart district, June 5, 2023 (attached to the Hamill letter; mother's name redacted): the mother of an Academy of the Canyons student "claims her daughter was being 'secretly socially transitioned' with the 'possible help' of a biology teacher"; "It appears that the student (born a female) is now identifying as a male and is using a new name at school." — [Hamill letter, exhibit memo p. 1](https://www.the74million.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/CaliforniaFERPAandTitleIXUSDOELTR1.31.25-1-1.pdf)
- **[records]** Same memo: "The general rule, therefore, is that the District cannot inform a parent about a student's social transitioning without the student's consent, unless an exception applies"; and "We are not aware if any health/safety conditions existed in this instance that would have created an exception." — [Hamill letter, memo p. 6](https://www.the74million.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/CaliforniaFERPAandTitleIXUSDOELTR1.31.25-1-1.pdf)
- **[records]** Same memo: parents' right to review classwork "becomes clouded ... if the classwork could reveal a student's gender identity/expression"; on May 11, 2023 she asked to agendize a Genspect-drafted gender policy and counsel advised the board need not agendize it; she met Supt. Kuhlman on June 1, 2023. — [Hamill letter, memo pp. 1–2, 6](https://www.the74million.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/CaliforniaFERPAandTitleIXUSDOELTR1.31.25-1-1.pdf)
- The memo records the mother's claims and the district's legal position. It does not admit that any employee initiated or encouraged the transition.

**(c) What rests on the mother's account (corroborated in part, otherwise allegation)**
- **[corroborated]** Fall 2021 (9th grade, first time in public school after homeschooling): she found journals with self-harm imagery and notes about a binder, top surgery and a male name; she and her husband told the child they would not affirm. Near the end of 9th grade (2022) she saw a male first name on a biology assignment; the teacher called it a nickname. — [The 74, 2025-06-09](https://www.the74million.org/article/the-california-mom-at-the-center-of-trumps-crackdown-on-school-gender-policies/); [Substack, 2026-11-15](https://lydia833.substack.com/p/parent-panel-for-segm)
- **[reported]** The principal refused to meet, citing AB 1266; the district denied her records request for a "secret social transition" plan; an English essay was withheld for months, then released on counsel's advice. — [The 74, 2025-06-09](https://www.the74million.org/article/the-california-mom-at-the-center-of-trumps-crackdown-on-school-gender-policies/)
- **[reported, hearsay from the child]** The principal told the child she was "under a district mandate not to tell" the mother and that teachers could lose their jobs. Our Duty's version: the principal told the child the identity "would be their secret." — [Substack, 2026-11-15](https://lydia833.substack.com/p/parent-panel-for-segm); [Our Duty, 2024-09-22](https://ourduty.group/2024/09/22/what-happens-when-schools-secretly-socially-transition-a-child/)
- **[reported]** She says she holds "nearly a thousand" internal emails involving teachers, the superintendent, assistant superintendent, equity staff and a teachers' union leader. These are not published. — [Substack, 2026-11-15](https://lydia833.substack.com/p/parent-panel-for-segm)
- **[reported]** Binder: "An older transgender-identified girl gave my daughter a breast binder on school grounds." This is a peer, not staff. — [Substack, 2026-11-15](https://lydia833.substack.com/p/parent-panel-for-segm)

Per the mother, the child signed a statement directing use of her given name and the district agreed in writing to stop. The child graduated in June 2025 and identifies as a girl. — [The 74, 2025-06-09](https://www.the74million.org/article/the-california-mom-at-the-center-of-trumps-crackdown-on-school-gender-policies/); [Substack, 2026-11-15](https://lydia833.substack.com/p/parent-panel-for-segm)
- The child's contrary perspective (from the withheld essay): the mother's response to her coming out at about 11 was "dismissive and critical." The child has not been interviewed. — [The 74, 2025-06-09](https://www.the74million.org/article/the-california-mom-at-the-center-of-trumps-crackdown-on-school-gender-policies/)

**(d) District response**
- Spokeswoman Debbie Dunn declined to address the case and said officials would "continue to follow the laws and procedures applicable to the district." — [The 74, 2025-06-09](https://www.the74million.org/article/the-california-mom-at-the-center-of-trumps-crackdown-on-school-gender-policies/)

**(e) Classification**
- Parental involvement: **parents not notified** (2021–22); then **conduct continued after explicit parental objection** per the mother (written non-consent to the name change; 2022–23); both parents declined to affirm. Consent relevant because the student was 14–16.
- Medical: **no medication or surgery; no staff referral alleged in any version.** The journal references to hormones and mastectomy are the child's wishes, not treatment.
- Four-element test: (1) social transition, supported by the memo's "using a new name at school"; (2) **no**; (3) yes initially; (4) n/a. **Not met.**
- No lawsuit and no formal FERPA complaint by the mother: she "ultimately got the records she was seeking after threatening to sue the district." — [The 74, 2026-01-29](https://www.the74million.org/article/ed-dept-says-california-violated-law-by-concealing-students-gender-identity/)
